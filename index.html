Three ways to make mathematics interactive

PROJECT 01

Function Factory

Quadratic transformations through guided discovery

A Desmos learning experience where learners manipulate visual controls to discover how a, h, and k transform a parabola. The experience moves from exploration to prediction, combined transformations, a final challenge, and mathematical explanation.

DESMOS ALGEBRA DIRECT MANIPULATION GUIDED DISCOVERY
Design focus: Learners encounter each transformation through interaction before formalizing the corresponding rule.

PROJECT 02

Beat the Odds

Probability, simulation, and expected value

Learners choose between competing probability machines, collect their own experimental data, scale simulations from 10 to 10,000 trials, and investigate why winning more often does not necessarily lead to the greatest expected payoff.

JAVASCRIPT PROBABILITY SIMULATION DECISION MAKING
Design focus: The formal expected-value calculation is withheld until learners have generated and interpreted enough evidence to need it.
LAUNCH BEAT THE ODDS
PROJECT 03

Shape Lab

Geometry through visual investigation

Learners rebuild the triangle area formula through measurement, cutting, direct manipulation, and formula verification. A draggable vertex experiment makes the relationship between base, perpendicular height, and area visible before the formula is formalized.

JAVASCRIPT GEOMETRY VISUAL REASONING DIRECT MANIPULATION
Design focus: Learners verify the formula by testing multiple cases and trying to break an incorrect rule rather than simply being told which formula is correct.

LEARNING DESIGN APPROACH

Interaction should reveal the mathematics.

Across my work, I use interaction as a tool for thinking rather than decoration. Each mechanic is designed to expose a relationship, generate a question, or help learners test an idea.

Explore Before Explain

Learners encounter a mathematical relationship through a problem, visual, or interaction before receiving the formal rule.

Make Thinking Visible

Predictions, explanations, choices, and reflections reveal how learners are reasoning rather than measuring only final answers.

Scaffold Toward Independence

Experiences begin with constrained exploration and gradually remove supports as learners build confidence and transferable understanding.

Formalize After Discovery

Mathematical notation and formulas arrive after learners have developed an intuitive need for them through evidence and pattern recognition.
